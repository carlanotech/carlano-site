Current work

Two projects, one principle

Carlano is focused. Each project takes a real source of waste — physical or computational — and engineers it down.

Hardware · Automation

Sustainable Pump System

An automated dispensing system for bulk and refill retail. By making pump-based dispensing reliable and self-serve, it removes friction from buying without packaging — so single-use plastic stops being the default.

Goal: make zero-waste refill as easy as grabbing a packaged product.
AI · Sustainable compute

Cloud2GroundAI

A bridge that lets a cloud assistant delegate heavy, mechanical work to a capable local model running on the user's own machine. The expensive token generation happens on the ground — off of datacenters and on your own local machine — quieter on cost, lighter on the grid, and clear about when delegation actually helps.

About

A small shop with a clear aim

Carlano Technology Solutions LLC is an independent engineering practice founded on a simple belief: good engineering should leave the planet and the people on it better off than it found them.

That means choosing projects where the engineering itself does the good — not as an afterthought, but as the point. Less plastic. Less wasted computer power. Systems that are honest about their trade-offs and built to actually ship.

Right now that focus is two projects: the Sustainable Pump System and Cloud2GroundAI. Deliberately few, done well.

How we work

  • Pick problems where the engineering is the environmental win.
  • Build one of each thing, and build it right.
  • Be honest about what helps and what doesn't.
  • Stay small enough to stay focused.
