What it is

Cloud2GroundAI is a bridge that lets a cloud assistant delegate heavy, mechanical work to a capable local model running on the user's own machine. The expensive token generation happens on the ground — quieter on cost, lighter on the grid, and clear about when delegation actually helps.

Why it matters

  • Moves token-heavy generation off remote datacenters and onto local hardware that can run on renewable power.
  • Lowers cost and energy use without giving up the judgment of a capable cloud assistant.
  • Built-in knowledge of when to delegate and when not to — delegation only when it actually helps.
  • Keeps the work transparent and reviewable rather than a black box.
